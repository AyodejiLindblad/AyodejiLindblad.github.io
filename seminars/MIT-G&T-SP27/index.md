Welcome to the website for the Spring 2027 iteration of the MIT Geometry & Topology Seminar, organized by [Joye Chen](https://math.mit.edu/directory/profile.html?pid=2584), [Ayodeji Lindblad](https://low-dim.top), and [Tom Mrowka](https://math.mit.edu/~mrowka/). The seminar meets on Mondays from 3:30PM - 4:30PM (50 minutes + questions) in room 449 of [MIT Building 2](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7qUtncvjbaUnE5SH9?g_st=ic). You can use [this link]() to add all seminar events to your Google Calendar.
